About

Games have always been the escape

They were my escape right from school, and that is what pushed me to find out how they are actually put together.

I am a game developer, programmer and VR developer based in Bangalore, India. Three years of that time has been spent inside the Unity engine, two of them building games end to end.

Right now I am an SDE-II at AutoVRse, where I work with clients from the first conversation about what they need through to shipping VR experiences that hold up on performance, visuals and usability. Before that I was at PlayShifu, building multiple Unity titles for the Terra platform along with the under-the-hood editor tooling that kept those projects moving.

Most of what I enjoy sits in the structure of a project — game programming, technical design, and the patterns that keep a codebase workable once it grows. Outside of that you will find me gaming, reading, riding or travelling.
